## Post-run listening repair — 2026-08-11

The listening audit accepted all 14 join warnings. A separate retry-list item,
`ch07_0223`, was not intelligible and sounded like “zero mirrors.” The authored
line is `"Oh, Mirris,"`.

The defect was deterministic: the final retry used Whisper's scoring
normalizer as TTS input. That normalizer maps the interjection `oh` to the
number word `zero`; Whisper then normalized both sides back to the same form
and incorrectly reported WER 0. The repair:

- uses punctuation-only, lexically preserving text preparation for TTS retries;
- fails `oh → zero` as a semantic transcription mismatch even when normalized
  WER would otherwise be zero;
- keeps retry WAV and speaker-embedding `.pt` sidecars together during cleanup
  and promotion, preventing stale similarity scores;
- includes unique mid-sentence proper names such as `Mirris` in book-local ASR
  matching without relaxing ordinary-word validation; and
- lays out quality-attempt status, attempt, WER, reason, and audio in separate
  responsive columns so long status values no longer overlap.

The old WAV and its embedding/manifests were preserved under
`brain/projects/sample_book-14/repair-backups/ch07_0223-zero-mirris/`. The
checkpointed repair synthesized only the affected line on its first pass; a
fail-closed name-spelling result then caused a cached chapter-7 revalidation.
The final candidate transcribed the name as `O'Mearis`, passed only through the
explicit `Mirris` glossary rule, and retained speaker similarity 0.9704. Chapter
7 was remastered and the full M4B re-exported. A final human check of this one
replacement clip remains required.
